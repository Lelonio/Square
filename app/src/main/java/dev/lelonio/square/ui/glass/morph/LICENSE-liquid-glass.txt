The morph physics (LiquidMorphPhysics.kt) and the SDF scene in the morph
shader (MorphGlass.kt) are ported from liquid_glass_widgets:

  https://github.com/sdegenaar/liquid_glass_widgets

whose rendering engine is derived from liquid_glass_renderer:

  https://github.com/whynotmake-it/flutter_liquid_glass/tree/main/packages/liquid_glass_renderer
