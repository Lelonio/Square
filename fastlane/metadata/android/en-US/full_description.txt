Square is a free, open-source Android music client for Spotify Premium and YouTube Music, with a Liquid Glass interface.

• Synced lyrics and Canvas
• Spotify Connect
• Independent speed and pitch controls, reverb and effect presets
• Local music files and playlist support
• New releases from artists you follow
• Per-playlist sorting
• Sleep timer

Spotify playback requires a Spotify Premium account. Setup instructions are included in the app. Some playback modes have different capabilities; audio effects are not available through Spotify Web Playback.

Square uses proprietary Spotify and YouTube Music services. Optional Spotify Web Playback loads Spotify's official JavaScript SDK remotely in a WebView; it is not bundled in the APK. Square is not affiliated with Spotify or Google.
